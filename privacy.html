PRIVACY & TRUST

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026 · Demo website policy

1. Who we are

MIT CIB is presented as an independent private cybersecurity awareness initiative. It is not Nepal Police, the Central Investigation Bureau, the Cyber Bureau or a government organization.

2. This is a static demo

The report and contact forms on this version run only in your browser. They display a local preview and do not send form entries to MIT CIB or save them to a server. No real reporting service is connected.

3. Do not submit sensitive information

Do not enter passwords, one-time passwords (OTPs), PINs, full payment-card numbers, recovery codes, identity documents or other secrets. Do not upload private information about another person.

4. External websites

Links to external websites, including official Nepal Police resources, are provided for convenience. Their own privacy policies and terms apply when you visit them.

5. Future versions

Before enabling real submissions, MIT CIB must publish accurate contact details, explain what information is collected and why, identify who can access it, set retention and deletion rules, use secure transmission and storage, and obtain any required legal advice and consent.

6. Contact

This demo does not yet publish a verified contact email or postal address. Contact details should be added only after they are established and monitored.

ImportantThis page is a starter policy for a demo, not legal advice and not a substitute for a Nepal-specific privacy compliance review before collecting real customer data.
