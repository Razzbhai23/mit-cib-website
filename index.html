01 — ABOUT MIT CIB

Cybersecurity with responsibility.

MIT CIB is an independent, private cybersecurity initiative focused on education, awareness and authorized security support.

MIT.

Our mission is to make digital safety easier to understand and practice. We help people recognize common online threats and make more informed decisions.

03 — KNOW THE RISKS

Cyber alerts & safety notes.

General awareness examples. These are educational notes, not verified reports of a current incident.

SCAM AWARENESS

Unexpected prize messages

Be cautious if a message says you won a prize but asks for a fee, OTP or banking details to claim it.

SAFETY NOTE / 01
ACCOUNT SECURITY

Never share your OTP

A genuine support agent should not ask you to disclose your one-time password or recovery code.

SAFETY NOTE / 02
SOCIAL MEDIA

Verify urgent requests

If a friend asks for money from a new account or number, verify through a separate trusted channel first.
